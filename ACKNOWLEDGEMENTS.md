# Astromigos credits and provenance

Astromigos is an original project by the Astromigos team. No external inspiration is claimed.

## Required third-party attribution

The anatomy assets are derived from Z-Anatomy and BodyParts3D and are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Their source attribution, license, modifications, and exclusions are documented in [the anatomy asset license](frontend/web/public/anatomy/LICENSE.md). This attribution is required for those assets and is not a claim of project inspiration.

The Artemis II demonstration uses public crew names, roles, and portraits sourced from NASA. See [the crew image credits](frontend/web/public/crew/CREDITS.md). All profile telemetry and medical histories in the demo are simulated; they do not represent the astronauts' actual health.

The included NASA OSDR reference datasets retain their source metadata and provenance in `data/`. Software dependencies remain subject to their respective licenses.

## Astromigos artwork

The Astromigos Team artwork was supplied by the Astromigos team. No separate public reuse license for that artwork is asserted.
